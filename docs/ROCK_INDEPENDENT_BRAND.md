# ROCK — Independent Brand Direction

## Position
ROCK is a standalone consumer-tech brand built around everyday technology accessories. Rehab Ajyad is the commercial parent context; the customer-facing brand experience is ROCK.

## Product world
Charging · Power · Car · Audio

The storefront should feel like one product ecosystem, not a mixed accessories marketplace.

## Brand promise
**POWER YOUR EVERYDAY.**

ROCK exists to make everyday technology simpler to power, protect, connect and use.

## Personality
Confident, engineered, clear, modern and practical.

Avoid luxury-for-luxury language, excessive technical jargon, visual clutter and decorative effects that compete with the product.

## Visual direction
- Core: black / off-white / warm paper.
- Signal accent: electric lime used sparingly for CTAs, states, highlights and selected product moments.
- Typography: bold geometric display for brand statements, readable Arabic UI for commerce.
- Imagery: product-first, high contrast, generous negative space, close-up material details.
- Motion: slow product float, reveal transitions, subtle hover elevation, horizontal marquees and meaningful cart/search feedback.
- Logo: use only the supplied official ROCK logo assets. Do not recreate, recolor into a different mark, or place a second/fake logo over it.

## Digital experience
Home → World/category → Product → Cart → Checkout → Support.

The homepage should establish the brand in the first screen, then move into products quickly. Product cards must remain transactional and readable even when motion is enabled.

## Content architecture
### Hero
POWER YOUR EVERYDAY.
A short Arabic value proposition and a direct route to the product catalog.

### Worlds
Power / Charging
Car / Motion
Audio / Sound

### Standard
Explain why ROCK: clear specs, useful design, practical performance and a simple buying journey.

### Brand story
Present ROCK as an independent customer-facing technology brand without misrepresenting corporate ownership or inventing history.

## Commerce rules
- Never invent a price, warranty, stock status, delivery estimate or contact channel.
- Use catalog data as the source of truth for current products.
- Keep request-only products clearly identified.
- Do not imply that GitHub Pages currently processes card payments.

## Current implementation target
Phase 1 is brand + storefront foundation. The existing static catalog, search, filters, cart and bilingual layer remain in place while the visual system becomes consistently ROCK-owned.

## Source context
The current Rehab Ajyad corporate site describes smart technology and accessories as a core business area and emphasizes innovation, quality and support. ROCK should inherit those product-quality principles while presenting a distinct customer-facing identity. See the live corporate site: https://rehabajyad.com/
